FAQ

Still have questions?

What is Orino Space?
Orino Space is the company behind two AI video tools for creators: Orino Studio (translate, dub, and re-edit footage you already have) and Orino Flow (produce brand-new video from nothing but an idea).
Should I choose Orino Studio or Orino Flow?
If you already have a video and want to translate, dub, or edit it, pick Studio. If you don't have a video yet and want AI to generate a brand-new one from an idea, pick Flow. Many creators use both for different parts of their workflow.
Can I use both products at the same time?
Yes. Studio and Flow are two separate desktop apps, purchased and installed independently, but they share the same support team — one place to reach out for either product.
Is my video and data processed locally?
Both products prioritize local processing wherever possible — your video/audio doesn't leave your machine unless it's actually needed. For steps that require generative AI (external AI translation, image/video generation, etc.), content is sent to whichever AI provider account you connect yourself (Gemini, OpenAI, etc.).
Is there a free trial?
Yes — both products include a trial period so you can try the app before purchasing a plan. Check each product's own pricing page for details.
Where do I get support?
The same support team covers both products — reach out via the Zalo/Telegram groups or the contact page, whether you use Studio or Flow.
